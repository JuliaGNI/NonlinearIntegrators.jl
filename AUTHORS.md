# Authors

NonlinearIntegrators' development is coordinated by a group of *principal developers*, who are also its main contributors and who can be contacted in case of questions about NonlinearIntegrators. In addition, there are *contributors* who have provided substantial additions or modifications. Together, these two groups form "The NonlinearIntegrators Authors" as mentioned in the [LICENSE](LICENSE.md) file.
